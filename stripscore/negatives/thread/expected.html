Why does my sourdough starter smell like acetone?

marta posted on 2026-03-02

My starter is about ten days old. Until yesterday it smelled pleasantly sour, like yogurt. This morning it smells sharp, almost like nail polish remover. I feed it once a day with equal weights of flour and water. Is it dead, or is this normal at some stage?

ove replied on 2026-03-02

That acetone smell is the starter telling you it is hungry. Once the yeast and bacteria run out of food they start producing alcohol and other volatile compounds, which is what you are smelling. It is not dead. Feed it twice a day for a few days, or move it somewhere cooler so it burns through the flour more slowly.

marta replied on 2026-03-03

Thanks. I switched to two feedings and the smell is already milder. Should I also change the ratio, or is once-a-day fine again once it settles?

dinh replied on 2026-03-03

Ratio matters more than frequency in my experience. A 1:5:5 feed keeps mine happy for a full day at room temperature, while 1:1:1 needs feeding every eight hours in summer. If you bake once a week, keep it in the fridge and feed it the day before you need it.

ove replied on 2026-03-04

Agreed with dinh. One more thing: a thin layer of grey liquid on top is the same story. Pour it off or stir it in, either is fine, then feed.
